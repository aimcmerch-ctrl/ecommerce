ABOUT AIMCMERCH

AIMCMERCH was launched in 2025, debuting with our winter merchandise under the supervision and vision of CEO Shahab Zafar. Created to strengthen the deep attachment to the soul of our college legacy, AIMC Merch unites talented student designers, models, and visionaries to embody the pride and brotherhood of Allama Iqbal Medical College. We strive to provide premium quality attire that truly resonates with the academic prestige and poetic lineage of our institution.
